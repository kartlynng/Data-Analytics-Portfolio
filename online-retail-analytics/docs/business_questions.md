# 📊 Business Analytics Questions Framework

This document outlines the **14 core business questions** addressed in the **UCI Online Retail Analytics Project**. Queries are categorized across 4 key operational domains to drive data-informed strategies for revenue optimization, customer retention, inventory management, and global expansion.

---

## 📈 1. Revenue & Sales Performance Trends
* **Q1: High-Level Business KPI Performance**  
  * *Objective:* Calculate baseline operational metrics—total net revenue, overall order volume, active customer count, Average Order Value (AOV), and average items per order.

* **Q2: Monthly Revenue & Order Growth**  
  * *Objective:* Evaluate month-over-month sales velocity and order volume to identify seasonal demand patterns across the historical dataset.

* **Q3: Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth Rates**  
  * *Objective:* Compute the percentage growth or contraction in monthly net revenue to measure business trajectory over time.

* **Q4: Sales Concentration by Peak Days & Hours**  
  * *Objective:* Analyze order timing by day of the week and hour of the day to identify peak purchasing windows for targeted promotional campaigns and server scaling.

---

## 👥 2. Customer Segmentation & Purchasing Behavior
* **Q5: VIP Customer Identification**  
  * *Objective:* Rank the Top 10 highest-spending customers to evaluate lifetime value (LTV) and purchasing frequency among key accounts.

* **Q6: Customer Spend Distribution (Pareto 80/20 Rule)**  
  * *Objective:* Segment customer accounts into deciles to measure revenue concentration and verify if top customer tiers generate the majority of total revenue.

* **Q7: Purchase Frequency & Loyalty Tiers**  
  * *Objective:* Categorize buyers into loyalty cohorts (Single-Purchase, Regular, Loyal, VIP Champions) to determine overall customer retention health.

* **Q8: Average Inter-Purchase Interval**  
  * *Objective:* Measure the average time gap (in days) between consecutive orders for repeat buyers to inform optimal email re-engagement timing.

---
## 📦 3. Product & Inventory Analytics

* **Q9: Best-Selling Products by Revenue & Volume**  
  * *Objective:* Identify the Top 10 revenue-generating and highest-volume SKUs to optimize inventory stocking levels and supply chain prioritization.

* **Q10: Low-Performing SKU Identification**  
  * *Objective:* Isolate bottom-tier SKUs ($<\$50$ lifetime sales) to flag slow-moving inventory for potential clearance, discounting, or catalog removal.

* **Q11: Market Basket Analysis (Co-Purchased Products)**  
  * *Objective:* Identify item pairs most frequently purchased together in the same order to inform cross-selling rules and bundle pricing strategy.

---
## 🌐 4. Geographic & Cross-Border Market Performance

* **Q12: Top International Markets (Excluding Domestic UK)**  
  * *Objective:* Compare foreign market revenue, order volume, and customer count to pinpoint high-growth overseas expansion targets.

* **Q13: Domestic vs. International Revenue Split**  
  * *Objective:* Measure overall market dependence by analyzing the revenue percentage split between United Kingdom sales vs. international sales.

* **Q14: Revenue per Customer across Nations**  
  * *Objective:* Compute the average spend per customer by country (filtering for statistical significance) to locate high-purchasing-power markets.
